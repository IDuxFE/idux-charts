# Code of Conduct / 行为准则

Everyone participating in iDux Charts issues, pull requests, and other project
spaces is expected to help maintain a respectful and welcoming community.

参与 iDux Charts 的 Issue、PR 和其他项目社区空间时，请共同维护尊重、友好的交流环境。

- Discuss ideas and code constructively; explain disagreements with evidence.
- Welcome different backgrounds and levels of experience, and help newcomers.
- Respect personal boundaries and protect other people's private information.
- Do not harass, discriminate, threaten, insult, or publish sexualized content.
- Do not post spam or repeatedly disrupt discussions.

- 围绕想法和代码建设性讨论，使用事实说明分歧。
- 尊重不同背景和经验水平，帮助新参与者。
- 尊重个人边界，保护他人隐私。
- 禁止骚扰、歧视、威胁、侮辱或发布色情内容。
- 禁止垃圾信息或反复扰乱讨论。

Report conduct concerns privately to the IDuxFE maintainers at
<idux.feteam@gmail.com>, including links and relevant context. Maintainers should
protect reporters' privacy, consider the available evidence, and explain
moderation decisions to the people involved where appropriate. Responses may
include a request to change behavior, a warning, removal of content, or temporary
or permanent restrictions on participation, depending on severity and repetition.

行为问题请私下发送至 IDuxFE 维护者邮箱 <idux.feteam@gmail.com>，提供链接和相关上下文。
维护者应保护报告者隐私，结合证据处理，并在适当时向相关人员说明管理决定。
根据严重程度和重复情况，处理可包括要求调整行为、警告、移除内容，或临时、永久限制参与。
